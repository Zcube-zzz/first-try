## Introduction

Welcome to ResumeFit AI. We provide resume upload, role-fit diagnosis, AI resume rewriting, and fact-confirmation guidance. We respect your privacy and minimize storage of raw resumes, job descriptions, and job-search content.

## Information We Process

We may process the following information:

1. **Account Information**
   - Name, email address, and account identifiers used for sign-in and account management.

2. **Resume And Role Content**
   - PDF, DOCX, image, or text resumes you submit.
   - Target roles, role directions, or job descriptions you provide.
   - This content is used to parse resumes, diagnose fit, and generate optimized resume drafts.

3. **Usage And Device Information**
   - Page visits, button interactions, error categories, file size, request time, browser type, device type, and language.
   - We use this information to improve reliability and product experience.

4. **Payment And Billing Information**
   - If paid plans are enabled, billing and transaction information may be handled by third-party payment providers.

## Resume And JD Handling

In V1, uploaded files and text are processed for the current request. We do not store raw resumes, raw JDs, or complete optimized resume bodies by default. We do not use your uploaded content to train models.

If AI infers metrics, scope, outcomes, or facts from context, those items are separated into a facts-to-confirm list so you can verify them before use.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. To provide diagnosis and rewriting, necessary content from your request may be sent to the configured AI service provider. We may also process information when required by law, to prevent abuse, or to maintain service security and reliability.

## Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, and minimized logs to protect information. No internet transmission or electronic storage method is perfectly secure, so avoid uploading sensitive information unrelated to your job search.

## Your Choices

You can paste redacted resume text instead of uploading files. You can stop using the service or contact us about account-related data requests.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.
